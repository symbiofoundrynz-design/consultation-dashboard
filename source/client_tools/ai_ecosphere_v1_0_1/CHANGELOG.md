# AI EcoSphere Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Replaced the idle return-and-stop behaviour with slow continuous cloud rotation after 10 seconds of inactivity.
- Idle rotation pauses on node hover, selection, drag, open information sheet, reduced-motion preference, or manual motion disable.
- Added configurable `idleRotationDelayMs` and `idleRotationSpeedRadiansPerSecond` values.
- Preserved the visual engine, data adapter contract, assets, node hierarchy, and interaction model unchanged.

## 1.0.0

- Initial standalone AI EcoSphere release.
